Before your visit

We hold your appointment for 15 minutes. If you're running late, call us and we'll do our best to fit you in, though we may need to shorten your service.

Cancellations or changes with less than 24 hours' notice are charged at 50% of the service price. Missed appointments are charged in full.

Colour services need a patch test at least 48 hours before your first appointment. Pop in any time we're open; it takes five minutes.
