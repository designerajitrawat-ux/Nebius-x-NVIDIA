Questions, answered

What happens when my trial ends?

We'll email you three days before. If you don't cancel, your card is charged for the plan you picked, for the number of members in your workspace.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades apply straight away; downgrades apply at the end of your billing period.

Can I use Fernhill for free?

Yes. Fernhill Free covers up to 3 projects and 5 members, with no card required.

Do you offer discounts for charities?

Registered charities and schools get 50% off any paid plan. Contact us after you sign up.
