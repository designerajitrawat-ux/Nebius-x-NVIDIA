## Title

FiveUsers: 5 AI users test your website before real ones do | NVIDIA Nemotron on Nebius

## Description

Before you ship, you should watch five people try your product. Almost nobody does, because recruiting them takes days. FiveUsers does it in a few minutes: paste a link, say what to try, and five AI users with different backgrounds try your website in real browsers, think out loud, and tell you exactly what to fix.

▶ Try it: https://fiveusers.vercel.app
▶ Sample reports: https://fiveusers.vercel.app/samples
▶ Code (MIT): https://github.com/designerajitrawat-ux/Nebius-x-NVIDIA

What you see in this video
• Five testers (a first-timer, someone in a hurry, a phone-only user, an older user with less digital confidence and a non-native English reader) use a sample signup flow at the same time, each in their own browser.
• Their faces and confusion meters change step by step, and three of them get stuck.
• The report: who finished and who didn't, every issue pinned on the real screenshot, quotes in the tester's own words, a concrete fix with a before and after view, and how other products solve the same step.
• The live run is real and sped up 3.5x. Nothing is scripted.

How it works
• NVIDIA Nemotron on Nebius Token Factory runs every step: Nemotron 3.5 Lightning reads the site, Nemotron 3 Super writes the personas and decides each click, Nemotron 3.5 Super VL looks at every screenshot and confirms when the goal is reached, and Nemotron 3 Ultra writes the report.
• Tavily researches who uses this kind of product, and finds how other products handle each problem.
• About 130 model calls and about $0.10 per test.
